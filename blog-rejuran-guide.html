Rejuran skin boosters: the questions your customers will ask

September 24, 2026 · Miracle Healthcare editorial team

Few products walk into the consultation room by name. Rejuran does. Thanks to a decade of word-of-mouth from Korea, customers across Southeast Asia now ask for "the salmon injection" before the practitioner has said a word. Here are the questions they will ask — and the answers that set expectations correctly.

"What is it, exactly?"

Rejuran Healer by PharmaResearch is a polynucleotide (PN/PDRN) preparation derived from salmon DNA. Rather than filling or freezing anything, polynucleotides support the skin's own repair processes. The goal is skin quality: texture, elasticity, hydration and barrier recovery — not added volume.

"When will I see results?"

This is where honest framing wins repeat customers. Rejuran is a course treatment, not a one-visit transformation. A common structure is a series of sessions spaced two to four weeks apart, with changes in texture and glow building gradually across the course. Customers who expect a filler-style instant change will be disappointed; customers prepared for cumulative improvement become the product's best advocates.

"Does it hurt? What is the downtime?"

Intradermal injections across the face involve multiple small entry points, so clinics routinely apply a topical anesthetic such as EVE CAIN for 20–30 minutes beforehand. Temporary bumps and redness at injection points typically settle within a day or two — worth saying out loud so nobody panics on the evening of their first session.

"Who is it for?"

  • Thin, tired, dehydrated skin that makeup no longer sits well on
  • Early fine lines and enlarged pores
  • Post-procedure recovery — commonly combined after laser, RF or microneedling
  • Customers who explicitly do not want the "worked on" look of volume treatments

For the clinic: why it earns its menu slot

Rejuran anchors a "skin quality" menu tier between facials and injectables — a natural upgrade path for facial customers and a retention engine, since the course structure builds repeat visits. Pair the final session with a take-home ampoule such as NEURADERM Core Time and the ticket grows again.
