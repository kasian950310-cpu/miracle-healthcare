Cold chain 101: why a local warehouse changes everything

September 29, 2026 · Miracle Healthcare editorial team

Botulinum toxins and several skin boosters are labeled for refrigerated storage at 2–8 °C. That one line on the box decides more about where you should buy than any price list.

What happens in international transit

When professional products are shipped from Korea to Southeast Asia by parcel courier, the journey is typically three to seven days: airport handling, a flight, customs clearance, then domestic delivery — much of it in tropical heat. Sellers pack insulated boxes with ice packs, but ice packs are rated in hours, not days. It is common for overseas shops to state openly that products "may arrive warm" in summer and that this is not grounds for a refund. The temperature risk is transferred entirely to the buyer.

What a cold chain is supposed to look like

  • Storage: products held in monitored refrigeration from import until dispatch
  • Packing: insulated box and ice packs applied minutes before the courier collects — not days
  • Transit: hours within one country, not a week across borders
  • Handover: the buyer inspects on delivery, with a short window to report any issue with photos

The local warehouse difference

This is the structural reason we hold stock in Vietnam rather than dispatching from Korea. An order placed before 14:00 leaves our refrigerated warehouse the same day; Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi receive it the same or next day, other provinces within 1–3 business days. The insulated box only needs to survive hours — which is what insulated boxes are actually designed to do. There is no customs step for the buyer, no "warm arrival" clause, and transport damage or a wrong item is reshipped free of charge.

A buyer's checklist

  1. Ask where the product physically ships from — not where the company is registered.
  2. Read the refund policy for temperature language. "Warm arrival is normal" tells you everything.
  3. Check that lot numbers and expiry dates are printed on the box you receive.
  4. Inspect cold-chain parcels on delivery and photograph anything unusual immediately.
