About us

Authentic Korean aesthetic & medical products — stocked in Vietnam
Korean quality, delivered from around the corner.

Reputation. Safety. Real results.

We believe aesthetic medicine should be trustworthy, effective — and close to you. Miracle Healthcare imports Korean aesthetic and medical products through official channels, holds them in our own licensed warehouse in Vietnam, and delivers them to clinics, hospitals, spas and retail customers across the country.

Every item is sourced directly from the Korean manufacturer or its authorized exporter, travels with full import documentation, and is stored under the conditions the product requires — including a refrigerated cold chain for toxins and boosters. No gray-market parcels, no counterfeits, no two-week international wait.

Why choose us

Local warehouse, same-day dispatchOrders before 14:00 leave our Vietnam warehouse the same day. HCMC & Hanoi receive same-day or next-day; no customs, no import fees, no overseas cold-chain risk for the buyer.
100% authentic, fully documentedDirect sourcing from Korean manufacturers with invoices, import permits and product notifications. Lot numbers and expiry dates stay printed on every box.
Real B2B verification & supportProfessional (Rx) items are sold only to verified clinics, hospitals and registered spas. Our bilingual sales team supports your purchasing, documentation and after-sales questions on Zalo and WhatsApp.

Who we serve

Dermatology and plastic-surgery clinics, hospitals, aesthetic centers and spas buying at wholesale tiers — and individual customers buying cosmetics and home-care products at retail. Professional products are intended for use by trained, licensed practitioners only and are never sold for home use.
